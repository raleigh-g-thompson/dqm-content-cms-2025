## CPT Codes Summary

An audit of `input/vocabulary/valueset/external/` found **179 valuesets containing CPT codes**
(`http://www.ama-assn.org/go/cpt`):

- **91** declare CPT directly via `compose.include[].system`.
- **88** obtain CPT codes through `compose` references to other valuesets (CPT appears in their expansions).

**None of these 179 valuesets exceed 1000 codes.** The largest CPT valueset is
`2.16.840.1.113883.3.464.1003.1189-20230722` at **596** codes; every CPT-containing valueset's
`expansion.total` matches its `expansion.contains` count (no truncation). CPT valuesets are therefore
**not** affected by the 1000-code truncation defect, and none are among the 46 updated above.

## Total Codes Note

The `Total Codes` column reflects each valueset's `expansion.total`. 42 of the 46 updated valuesets
exceed 1000 codes; the four small valuesets (`FederalAdministrativeSex` = 2, `SmokingStatus` = 50,
`Race` = 6, `Ethnicity` = 2) are small by design.
