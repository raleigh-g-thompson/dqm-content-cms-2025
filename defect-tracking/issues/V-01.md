### V-01: Vendored `CumulativeMedicationDuration` dispense bug

- **Symptom**: `MedicationDispensePeriod`/`medicationDispensePeriod()` compute
  `daysSupply` via `(convert D.daysSupply to days).value`, which returns null on this
  engine (engine E-07); unlike the sibling MedicationRequest-side functions, the
  dispense-side was never patched by the library's own authors (their inline TODO:
  “this isn't working as expected, convert results in null”).
- **Resolution**: CMS128-local workaround function replicating the vendored logic minus
  the broken `convert`; `CMD.Quantity()` reused for the shared helper. Not patched
  upstream.
- **Category**: third-party / vendored CQL library.
