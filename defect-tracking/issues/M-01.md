### M-01: `doNotPerform` double-counted

- **Symptom**: a “do NOT perform” record was not excluded from `MedicationRequest`/
  `ServiceRequest` retrieves and was double-counted as an actual order.
- **Resolution**: fixed (CQL rework).
- **Category**: migration regression we introduced converting the CQL.
