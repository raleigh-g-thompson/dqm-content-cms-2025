### M-03: `.onset.toInterval()` vs `.prevalenceInterval()`

- **Symptom**: a zero-width onset point used for “chronic/still-active” checks can
  never `overlaps` a measurement period years later.
- **Resolution**: fixed (10 measures, ~16 call sites); required 4 attempts; final fix
  evolved into the E-13 base-retrieve generalization.
- **Category**: migration regression (interacts with engine E-03/E-05/E-06/E-13).
