### V-02: Vendored `CumulativeMedicationDuration` 6.0.000 model adaptation

- **Symptom**: full vendored `CumulativeMedicationDuration` 6.0.000 needed model
  adaptation beyond a drop-in copy: `timing.repeat.bounds as Interval<DateTime>` became
  `as FHIR.Period`; a net-new `ToDays(FHIR.Duration)` helper was needed; and
  `averageDailyDose()` had to bypass unit-aware Quantity division entirely.
- **Resolution**: fixed locally for CMS156 (deviations #2/#3/#4 in the vendor file
  header). Interacts with engine E-07/E-08/E-09.
- **Category**: third-party / vendored CQL library.
